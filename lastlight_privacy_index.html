Privacy Policy — Last Light

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Last Light Games ("we", "us") operates the mobile game Last Light (Android package com.lastlightgame.game). This policy describes what information the game does and does not collect, and why.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not operate our own player database and do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any free-text information. If you email us for support, we receive the address and contents of that email only.

2. Game saves — local and cloud (Unity Gaming Services)

Your game progress (upgrades, currency, settings, statistics) is saved on your device. The game also uses Unity Authentication and Unity Cloud Save (Unity Technologies) to keep a backup copy of that save so it can be restored or transferred:

  • Sign-in is anonymous by default — no name, email, or password. Unity assigns an anonymous player ID for your save.
  • The cloud copy holds the same game-progress data as your local save, nothing more. It is used only to back up and restore your progress; the newest save wins, and the cloud path never deletes your local save.
  • Optional linking: you may link a Google Play Games identity to the same player in Settings. Linking is your choice and protects your progress if you wipe or replace your device. Without linking, uninstalling the game or clearing its app data can orphan the anonymous cloud save.
  • To request deletion of your cloud save data, contact us at lastlightgamedev@gmail.com and we will delete the cloud copy associated with your player ID (shown in the game's Settings).

3. Advertising — Unity Ads

Last Light shows rewarded ads only — ads you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. There are no interstitial or banner ads. Showing an ad is handled by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), which may collect device information, an advertising identifier, IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

If you own the game's 2x Rewards Pack, the rewards those ads would grant are given instantly and no ad is requested for them.

4. Purchases — Google Play / Unity IAP

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment-card details. We receive only a purchase confirmation identifying the product bought (for example, a gem pack or a rewards pack) so the game can grant it.

5. Children

Last Light is not directed at children under 13.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Local game data: delete it any time by clearing the game's app data or uninstalling.
  • Cloud save data: email lastlightgamedev@gmail.com with your player ID (Settings) and we will delete the cloud copy.
  • Advertising data: reset or limit your device's advertising identifier in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Questions or requests: lastlightgamedev@gmail.com

7. Changes

If a future version adds a service that collects data (for example leaderboards), this policy will be updated and its date changed before that version is released.
